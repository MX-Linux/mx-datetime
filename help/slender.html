Using MX Date & Time as a desktop user

Launch MX Date & Time from the menu to see all three tabs. On the Date & Time tab, select a time zone to preview its local date and time. Choose Apply to save your changes.

The application requests administrator authorization through a helper when it needs access to system settings. Opening the Hardware Clock tab reads the clock and may prompt for authentication. The time-transfer and drift-adjust buttons act immediately. On the Network Time tab, Update Now saves the network-time settings and starts synchronization.
